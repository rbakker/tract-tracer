Tractogram Observer

An interactive, browser-based viewer to "poke around in the brain", combining 2D slice views with a versatile 3D renderer that contains both a volume viewer (ray marcher) and (iso)surface renderer for simultaneous viewing of a glass-like brain surface, atlas regions, streamlines with depth cues, brain slices and point markers.

Supports various probe types to interactively extract fiber bundles from multi-million streamline tractograms.

Created by Rembrandt Bakker in Claude.ai coworking mode, supported by Juelich Forschungszentrum (Helmholtz funding), TU Eindhoven (NWO grant number KICH1.ST03.21.004) and ETZ Tilburg.
